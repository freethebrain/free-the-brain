Free the Brain

This is you. Let it think about something else.

A task registry that holds the whole picture so you don't have to — every task, every deadline, every judgment you have made — and hands it back in doses small enough to face.

Use it in the browser

Google Play listing is in closed testing. The web app link is a placeholder domain until the real one is set.

Capture

One line, and it is recorded.

Type the task the way it came to you. It gets an ID, a date, and an Inbox status — nothing else is asked. Sorting it out is a separate act, on a separate day, when you have the attention for it.

From the phone, share any text from any app straight into the Inbox.

Triage

Small doses, in the right order.

A full-registry review is too heavy to face, so it never happens. Instead you get the next five or ten tasks that most need a judgment — overdue first, then hard deadlines, then the never-judged, then the stalest — and you can stop after any of them.

Urgency, importance, status, deadline: yours to set. The app only orders the queue.

Any AI

Claude, ChatGPT, whatever comes next.

The registry is an MCP server. Connect it to the assistant you already use and it can read the queue, stage a triage chunk, capture tasks from a conversation, and append notes — from a chat, by voice, on the move.

Connect your AI in five minutes →

The AI stages. You judge.

Every connected assistant works under one rule, enforced by the server rather than by good manners: it may read, order, chunk, flag, capture and take notes on its own, but it cannot change a priority, a status, a deadline or a category unless it is recording a decision you made in the conversation. A model that tries is refused. Your registry stays yours.
